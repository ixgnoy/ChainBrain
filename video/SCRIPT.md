# ChainBrain demo video: script and shot list

v3, 19 Oct 2026: as built. The final cut runs 3:58 (hard ceiling 4:55). Exact timings are in `remotion/src/timeline.json`; the voice-over text is in `tts/lines.json`.

- **Voice:** Kokoro (local) at about 140 wpm. The recommended voice is `af_heart`; the audition is `video/out/voice-audition.mp3`.
- **Subtitles:** burned-in English.
- **Music:** a cinematic build, composed in code from CC0 orchestral samples (VSCO 2 CE strings, brass and timpani) plus synth hits. The drop lands on Act 3.

**Phone footage** comes from the app's **built-in network** (Settings → Demo network: "On · the built-in simulation, no real chain"), filmed in one take with the app's own auto-play in the iOS Simulator.

**Console footage** is the web console on its built-in network, with the Judges arena and the Brain page.

**Truth rules:**
- Every phone and console shot carries a small tag: "built-in network · simulated chain".
- No hash from those shots is zoomed in on.
- The only hashes the video points at are the **11 real transactions on the hosted FISCO chain**, all checked live today. The video shows them in the hosted explorer and says so out loud.
