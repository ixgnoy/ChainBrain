# ChainBrain demo video: script and shot list

v7, 20 Oct 2026, as built: v4's story (the AI Card, with a "how it works" X-ray), the v4.2 script in Chin Jun Quan's cloned voice, and his own intro and last line on camera. 4:37.5 at 120 BPM (one beat = 15 frames). Why this story: [STORYBOARD-v4.md](STORYBOARD-v4.md). Exact timings: `remotion/src/timeline.json` (built from `remotion/src/edit.json`); voice-over text: `tts/lines.json`. The storyboard approved for this cut comes from `tools/board.py`.

- **USP:** ChainBrain gives your AI its own card. Its own smart contract sets the limits; a second AI watches every payment and can only make the rules stricter.
- **Voice:** Chin Jun Quan's own voice.
  - The narration is his ElevenLabs instant voice clone ("Quan", `eleven_v4`, `tts/eleven.py`), played 7% faster with the pitch kept; word timings come from the API's character timestamps.
  - The intro and the last line are his on-camera recordings (`tools/voice.py`).
  - The v4.2 wording adds the linking words people use when they talk ("First", "Then", "Once", "On top of that", "Meanwhile", "In other words") and keeps every claim of v4.1. It was reviewed against 2025-2026 research on AI-sounding writing.
- **Music:** a 120 BPM hybrid trailer cue composed in code (`music/score.py`): the Morse key, a slam on the 9%, a mechanism texture for the X-ray (bells for the keys, ticks for the checks, eight rising bells for the card), the drop on "Now, let's try to break it", half a second of silence before "The card says no", the mechanism again for the layers, D major for the team. Key hits punch through the voice ducking (`tools/mix.py`).
- **Picture:** real phone recordings (built-in network, tagged "recorded on our test setup"), the AI Card illustration, the X-ray board with real logos and app screens, the hosted explorer's real pages, Lucide icon badges, the team's photos, and Jun Quan's two clips in the film's phone frame with his words set large as he says them. No code on screen.
- **Subtitles:** burned in, English, 60 pages, at most 18.4 characters a second (all but one page at 17 or under). The two on-camera lines are set large beside the face instead; the `.srt` alongside keeps every line.
