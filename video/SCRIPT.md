# ChainBrain demo video: script and shot list

v5, 19 Oct 2026, as built (v3 edit, "the AI Card"). 3:33.0 at 120 BPM (one beat = 15 frames). Why this story: [STORYBOARD-v3.md](STORYBOARD-v3.md). Exact timings: `remotion/src/timeline.json` (built from `remotion/src/edit.json`); voice-over text: `tts/lines.json`.

- **USP:** ChainBrain gives your AI its own card. Its own smart contract sets the limits; a second AI watches every payment and can only make the rules stricter.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice Sarah (premade), generated with `tts/eleven.py` (key in `video/.env`); word timings from the API's character timestamps. Plain words: negotiate (not haggle), approve, limit, rejected, test setup.
- **Music:** a 120 BPM hybrid trailer cue composed in code (`music/score.py`): a Morse-key hook, a slam on the 9%, the drop on "Now, let's break it", half a second of silence before "No." and the biggest hit on it; key hits punch through the voice ducking (`tools/mix.py`, cues from `tools/cues.py`).
- **Picture:** real phone recordings (built-in network, tagged "recorded on our test setup"), the drawn AI Card (with the hosted proof card's real address tail and rules), real app UI crops, the hosted explorer's real pages, official marks on white plaques, the team's photos. No code on screen.
- **Subtitles:** burned in, English, ≤ ~17 characters a second; `.srt` alongside.
