v4, 19 Oct 2026, as built (v2 edit). 4:04 at 120 BPM (one beat = 15 frames). Exact timings: `remotion/src/timeline.json`; voice-over text: `tts/lines.json`.

- **Voice:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice Sarah (premade), generated with `tts/eleven.py` (key in `video/.env`); word timings from the API's character timestamps. Offline fallback: Kokoro.
- **Music:** a 120 BPM hybrid trailer cue composed in code (`music/score.py`, CC0 VSCO 2 CE samples plus synthesis); the drop lands on "Now, let's attack it"; hit points follow the voice (`tools/cues.py`).
- **Picture:** real phone recordings (built-in network, labelled on every phone and console shot), real app UI crops (`tools/crops.py`), real brand marks as small avatars beside the name (as in the app) and the official FISCO BCOS and x402 logos on white plaques, the Lucide icons the app uses, the hosted explorer's real transaction pages. No code on screen, no drawn diagrams.
- **Subtitles:** burned in, English, ≤ ~17 characters a second; `.srt` alongside.
