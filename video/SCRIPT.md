# ChainBrain demo video: script and shot list

v8, 20 Oct 2026, as built (v4.4): v4's story (the AI Card, with a "how it works" X-ray), the v4.2 script in Chin Jun Quan's cloned voice, his own intro and last line on camera, a section on the second AI, and the team as three university students from Malaysia. 4:44.5 at 120 BPM (one beat = 15 frames). Why this story: [STORYBOARD-v4.md](STORYBOARD-v4.md). Exact timings: `remotion/src/timeline.json` (built from `remotion/src/edit.json`); voice-over text: `tts/lines.json`. The storyboard approved for this cut comes from `tools/board.py`; before approval it was reviewed by three agents (a judge's eye, an accuracy audit against the code and the PDFs, a motion designer).

- **USP:** ChainBrain gives your AI its own card. Its own smart contract sets the limits; a second AI checks every payment and can only make the rules stricter.
- **Voice:** Chin Jun Quan's own voice.
  - The narration is his ElevenLabs instant voice clone ("Quan", `eleven_v4`, `tts/eleven.py`), played 7% faster with the pitch kept; word timings come from the API's character timestamps.
  - The intro and the last line are his on-camera recordings (`tools/voice.py`).
  - The wording uses the linking words people use when they talk ("First", "Then", "Once", "On top of that", "Meanwhile", "In other words"), checked against 2025-2026 research on AI-sounding writing.
- **Music:** a 120 BPM hybrid trailer cue composed in code (`music/score.py`): the Morse key, a slam on the 9%, a mechanism texture for the X-ray, the drop on "Now, let's try to break it", half a second of silence before "The card says no", the mechanism again for the layers, a bell as the Brain lights up and a low hit as the risky seller is cut off, D major for the team. Key hits punch through the voice ducking (`tools/mix.py`).
- **Picture:** real phone recordings (built-in network, tagged "recorded on our test setup"; the 20 Oct take was recorded after declining Claude's pending co-sign, so no ask dock shows), the AI Card illustration, the X-ray board with real logos and app screens, the hosted explorer's real pages, Lucide icon badges, the animated Brain graph, the team's photos with their universities' logos and the Malaysian flag, and Jun Quan's two clips in the film's phone frame with his words set large as he says them. No code on screen.
- **Subtitles:** burned in, English, balanced lines. Three lines are set large in the picture instead (the intro, the team headline, the last line); the `.srt` alongside keeps every line.
