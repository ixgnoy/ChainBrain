Voice recording kit (v4.1)

Highlighted lines changed in v4.1 for accuracy (video/overnight/research/script-v41.md). Read those words.

Your voice replaces the AI voice for every line. Read each file's lines in order; the times are targets, and anything within about 10% is fine. The video has about 8 seconds of room under the 5-minute limit, so keep a brisk, natural pace.

Setup

  • Phone voice recorder (iPhone Voice Memos is fine; set quality to Lossless if it offers it).
  • A small quiet room with soft things: curtains, bed, a wardrobe of clothes. Fans and air-con off, windows shut, phone on aeroplane mode.
  • Phone 15 to 20 cm from your mouth, a little to one side. Keep the same distance for every file.
  • Do a 10-second test and listen back on earphones first.

How to read

  • Talk as if you're explaining ChainBrain to one judge across a table. A little energy; smiling helps.
  • Leave about one second between lines.
  • Stumbled? Pause two seconds and read the whole line again. I keep the last take.
  • FISCO BCOS: "FIS-co B-C-O-S". x402: "x four oh two". HKMA: the letters.

Where the files go

  • Audio: video/footage/voice/, named as shown (01-hook.m4a and so on). Sending them here works too.
  • Camera clips: video/footage/face/ as intro.mov and close.mov (and team.mov if you film it).
  • Then tell me, and I rebuild the video around your takes.
