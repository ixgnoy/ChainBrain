## How things work now (keep all of it working)
- Both apps open on a **built-in network** every launch (`shared/sim`: virtual chain with the Pocket.sol rules, wallet tools, scripted agent, seeded history). Phone: `mobile/src/lib/builtin.ts`, `runtime.isBuiltin()`. Web: `web/src/source/*` (local / linked / live feeds). A quiet "Custom server" switch in Settings (phone) and the gear dialog (web) switches to a real server. Nothing on screen may say "demo", "mock" or "simulated".
- **Phone ↔ console link:** `pnpm --dir devstack relay` (or `pnpm --dir devstack start`). The phone mirrors its built-in network to the console; the console's Judges Play (`say`), plant and force-transfer go back to the phone (`devstack/src/relay.ts`, `mobile/src/lib/relay.ts`, `web/src/source/linkedFeed.ts`). The relay can never approve or sign; keep it that way.
- **Presenter mode:** console `#present` with a scene stepper that reads the phone's state.
