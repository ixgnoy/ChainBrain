## About the competition

The 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition — WeBank FinTech University Challenge is held with Shenzhen Municipal Local Financial Administration, Shenzhen University and WeBank jointly serving as strategic guidance units and the Shenzhen University–WeBank Institute of FinTech as host. The competition has been successfully held for eight consecutive editions, producing thousands of outstanding projects, attracting nearly 10,000 university students from China and abroad, and continuously advancing technological innovation in fintech, talent development, and industry–academia–research collaboration.

In response to the global momentum of artificial intelligence, the competition is comprehensively upgrading its International Track (Artificial Intelligence direction). With the 2026 APEC Summit set to take place in Shenzhen, the competition is seizing this opportunity to address the profound transformations that AI technologies are bringing to global economic and social development, upgrade its format with a more open international outlook, and expand recruitment to universities worldwide.

The competition has invited the CCF Technical Committee on Digital Finance, Tsinghua Institute of Financial Technology, IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Singapore Chapter and IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Shenzhen Chapter to serve as technical guidance units. A group of industry experts will serve as academic advisers and judges, providing participating teams with end-to-end professional support from competition format design through entry evaluation.

## Challenge: AI as a Financial Participant (International AI Track)

All directions address the theme "AI as a Financial Participant". Teams select one direction for in-depth implementation. Directions A–E are new or expanded this year; Direction F is the original Agent Wallet. Pick a direction you can handle and build a financial agent that can **understand, plan and execute safely** (「理解、规划、安全执行」).

| Direction | Name | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | AI Personal Finance Assistant | Conversational bookkeeping, budgeting and bill management |
| B | AI Wealth Assistant | Risk profiling, asset allocation and simulated investment planning |
| C | Cross-border / International Student Finance Assistant | Multi-currency, exchange rates, remittances and multilingual service (APEC feature) |
| D | AI Insurance Assistant | Policy interpretation, plan recommendations and claims guidance |
| E | Small-business Finance Assistant | Cash flow, reconciliation and operational recommendations |
| **F** | **Agent Wallet** (our direction) | An AI-owned wallet that can spend on-chain (实现真实链上花钱) |

The "Challenge Brief" (赛题解析) popup only restates the paragraph above and the list of directions.
