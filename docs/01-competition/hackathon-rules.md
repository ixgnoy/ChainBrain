# FinTechathon 2026 — International Track Rules

## English

### International Track Registration Rules

These rules apply to the International Artificial Intelligence Track.

1. **Eligibility** — The competition is open to full-time undergraduate, master's and doctoral students enrolled at overseas universities or research institutes, regardless of nationality, major or year of study. "Overseas universities" means higher education institutions and research institutes established outside China (including Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan), with independent educational qualifications and the ability to confer academic qualifications or degrees.

2. **Team formation** — Participation is team-based. Each team must have 2–5 members. Cross-year, cross-major, cross-university and cross-country teams are allowed. Non-Chinese nationals must account for at least 60% of each team. The Organising Committee will determine track eligibility based on members' nationalities, institutions and supporting documents. A team that does not meet the International Track requirements may be reassigned to the appropriate track or disqualified.

   Important note: To reflect the international character of the International Track, teams are encouraged to demonstrate diversity and international representation in their membership and to include students of different nationalities and cultural backgrounds. Where the calculation does not result in a whole number, the minimum is rounded up to the next whole person (for example, a 3-member team must include at least 2 non-Chinese members).

3. **Registration and information authenticity** — During registration, teams must truthfully provide information for every member, including name, country of nationality, type and number of a valid personal identification document such as a passport or other valid identity document, institution and proof of enrolment or student status, major, year of study and contact details. All information must be truthful, accurate and valid. Each person may join only one team across the Domestic and International Tracks; duplicate registration is invalid.

4. **Official language** — English is the official language of the International Track. Entry submission, presentations and judging are conducted in English, and submitted materials must include an English version.

5. **Independent completion and integrity** — Teams must complete their entries independently and comply with applicable laws and regulations. Cheating, fraud, plagiarism and infringement of others' intellectual property rights are prohibited. If a violation is discovered or reported and verified, the Organising Committee may revoke the team's eligibility and results.

6. **Compliance of entries and third-party materials** — Data, code, open-source components, APIs and other third-party materials used in an entry must be legally authorised and comply with applicable open-source licences and data-use terms. The team bears responsibility for infringement or improper use.

7. **Intellectual property** — The intellectual property rights of competition entries belong to the participants. The organiser may use entries and team information in promotional materials, publications, designated and authorised media, official website display and download, and exhibitions including touring exhibitions for non-commercial publicity. This licence is non-exclusive and royalty-free.

### Competition Process

Independent English-language submission and judging process. All dates use China Standard Time (UTC+8).

#### Submission & Selection

1. **Registration & Submission · 31 Aug–20 Oct** — Register by official email, select one of six topics and submit the required English materials by 24:00 on 20 October (UTC+8).
   - Technical documentation (Required)
   - Presentation deck, up to 10 minutes (Required)
   - Demo video, up to 5 minutes (Required)
   - Source code, security self-assessment and execution evidence (Optional)
2. **Compliance Screening · 21 Oct** — Formal review and International Track eligibility verification.
3. **Online Review · 22–23 Oct** — The judging panel selects ten finalist teams.

#### Finals

1. **Finalists & Invitations · 31 Oct** — The finalist list and formal invitation letters are issued.
2. **International Defence · 22 Nov** — The track defence and project presentation are conducted in English at WeBank in Shenzhen.
3. **Awards Ceremony · 23 Nov** — Awards are announced and selected projects are showcased.

### Judging Criteria

All six International Track topics use the same core judging dimensions.

| Weight | Criterion | What is assessed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | Task Completion | Completion of scripted tasks in a simulated environment for non-on-chain topics; **Topic F is evaluated using on-chain transaction hashes.** |
| 30% | Security & Compliance | Defence against induced transfers, data extraction, privilege escalation and prompt injection; serious security failures trigger substantial deductions. |
| 30% | Innovation & Interaction | Scenario novelty, real-world value, dialogue efficiency, clarification, correction and fallback capability. |

A capped 0–5 point challenge/implementation bonus rewards advanced topics or demonstrated real-world deployment. Topic choice itself does not affect the base score. Appeals may be filed within three business days after results are announced.

### Competition Statement

1. Participants must read and comply with the official competition rules, complete entries independently, and ensure that all submitted information and materials are truthful and lawful.
2. The Organising Committee may adjust the schedule, format, judging or defence arrangements according to actual circumstances and will notify teams through the official website, email or other official channels.
3. Entry intellectual property remains with participants. Use of entries and team information by the organiser is limited to the non-commercial competition publicity purposes described in these rules.
4. The International Track is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. The Chinese and English notices are published together; in case of discrepancy, the Chinese version prevails.

## 中文参考

### 国际赛道报名规则如下：

本规则适用于人工智能赛道（国际赛道）。

1. **参赛对象** — 本届竞赛面向国外高校及科研院所全日制在读学生（含本科生、硕士研究生、博士研究生），不限国籍、专业与年级。本规则所称"国外高校"，指设立在除中国（含香港、澳门、台湾地区）以外的国家，具有独立办学资质、可独立授予学历或学位的高等学校及科研机构。

2. **组队要求** — 须以团队形式参赛，每队 2—5 人，允许跨年级、跨专业、跨校及跨国组队。每支队伍中非中国籍成员占比须不低于 60%，组委会将结合队员国籍、就读院校及相关证明材料综合认定赛道归属。不符合国际赛道条件的，组委会有权调剂至相应赛道或取消参赛资格。

   温馨提示：为体现国际赛道的国际化特色，鼓励参赛团队在成员构成上体现多元化与国际化，支持不同国籍、不同文化背景的学生联合组队。人数计算结果不足一人时，按一人向上取整（例如，3 人团队须至少有 2 名非中国籍成员）。

3. **报名与信息真实性** — 报名时须如实填写全部队员信息（含姓名、所属国家（国籍）、有效个人证件类型及号码（护照或其他有效身份证件）、就读院校及在读证明（学籍信息）、专业、年级、联系方式），信息须真实、准确、有效。每人仅可加入一支队伍（国内赛道与国际赛道合计），重复报名无效。

4. **参赛语言（国际赛道）** — 本赛道官方语言为英文。作品提交、答辩与评审均以英文进行；提交材料应包含英文版本。

5. **独立完成与诚信** — 参赛队伍须独立完成作品，遵守相关法律法规，不得存在作弊、欺诈、抄袭或侵犯他人知识产权等行为。经发现或举报核实，组委会有权取消其参赛资格及成绩。

6. **作品与第三方素材合规** — 作品中使用的数据、代码、开源组件、API 及其他第三方素材，须合法取得授权并符合相应开源许可及数据使用条款；因作品侵权或违规使用产生的责任，由参赛队伍自行承担。

7. **知识产权** — 参赛作品知识产权归参赛选手所有。主办方有权将作品及队伍信息用于宣传品、出版物、指定及授权媒体发布、官网浏览与下载、展览（含巡展）等非商业性宣传用途。该授权为非排他、无偿授权。

### 评审规则

国际赛道六个方向共用统一核心评分维度，确保跨方向可比。

| 权重 | 维度 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | 任务完成率 | 非链上方向以模拟环境中的脚本化任务完成度为准；方向 F 以真实链上交易哈希为依据。 |
| 30% | 安全合规性 | 统一考察对诱导转账、信息套取、越权操作和 Prompt 注入的防御能力；严重安全问题将触发大幅扣分。 |
| 30% | 创新性及交互体验 | 考察场景新颖性、真实问题解决度、轮次效率、追问、纠错和兜底能力。 |

设置 0—5 分"挑战/落地加分"（封顶），奖励主动挑战高阶方向或实现真实落地的团队；方向选择本身不影响基础评分。评审结果公布后 3 个工作日内可提交书面异议。
