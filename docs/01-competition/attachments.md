Sources: `international-topics-rules-en.docx` (Attachment 2, "International Track — Topics & Rules") and `international-registration-form-en.xlsx` (Attachment 3, "Registration Form (English Only)"), both from the team's Downloads folder, read 19 Oct 2026. ## In one minute

- The attachments are **less authoritative than the website rules**: they are English-only, and the rules say the Chinese version prevails. But they are what the organisers will check the form and package against, so where they are stricter, follow them.
- They conflict with the website on **eight points** (table below). None can be settled without the organisers. For each we take the safer reading and, where cheap, do both.
- The registration form asks for much more than the team's plan assumed: **passport bio-page image, proof of enrolment, student ID number, age, gender, a team-leader signature, and a parental consent form for anyone under 18.**
- Deadline is **20 Oct 23:59 (Attachment 2) or 24:00 (website), UTC+8**. Today is 19 Oct. The team's own target (noon on 20 Oct) still holds.

## Attachment 2: what it adds

- **Theme:** "each team designs and builds an AI agent capable of completing real tasks in a financial scenario."
- **Topic F wording:** "The track's original on-chain topic — build an AI wallet agent that autonomously executes on-chain operations (asset management, transaction execution, risk control). Ambitious ideas are encouraged, with a 0–5 point challenge bonus." Scoring is the same for all six topics; choosing the on-chain topic does not change it.
- **Topic descriptions** (shorter and different from the website's directions):

| Topic | Attachment 2 wording |
| --- | --- |
| A Personal Finance Assistant | smart budgeting, bill analysis, spending insights, actionable recommendations from income and expenses |
| B Wealth Advisory Agent | product comparison, client risk profiling, advisory dialogue, compliant and explainable allocation |
| C Cross-border & Student Finance Assistant | remittances, tuition payments, credit building, multi-currency |
| D Insurance Assistant | policy Q&A, coverage-gap analysis, claims guidance |
| E SME Finance Copilot | financing-option matching, cash-flow management and alerts, business financial analysis |
| F Agent Wallet — Autonomous On-chain Wallet | autonomous on-chain operations: asset management, transaction execution, risk control |

- **Submission checklist (in full):**
  1. Technical documentation: system architecture, core algorithm description, security design
  2. Presentation deck: final presentation no longer than 10 minutes
  3. Demo video: no longer than 5 minutes (marked optional here, required on the website)
  4. Source code: README, GitHub repository link, deployment instructions, test cases
  5. Security self-assessment report: permission-tier implementation and known-risk list
  6. Execution evidence: Topic F needs real on-chain transaction hashes; other topics need sandbox logs and demo video
- **Resubmission:** allowed any time before the deadline, the latest version received prevails, nothing is accepted afterwards. It also promises a bilingual reminder email 24 hours before the deadline.
- **Benefits:** no fee; travel and accommodation for invited finalist members (number per team stated in the invitation); fast-track WeBank interviews for outstanding teams.
- **IP and integrity:** same as the website rules, "governed by the Competition Rules and Privacy Policy published on the official website". We have not seen a Privacy Policy page; note it if the organisers send one.

## Attachment 3: the registration form

Excel file with two sheets, "Instructions" and "Registration Form". One form per team.

**Team block** (A1–A7): Team Name\*, Topic (A–F)\*, Entry Title (provisional), Advisor (optional), Proposal Summary (optional, 100–200 words, "outlines your approach for the preliminary screening"), Team Email\*.

**Member table** (2–5 rows, row 1 is a grey example from a Malaysian student, overwrite it): Role\*, Full Name\*, Gender\*, Age\*, Country of Nationality\*, Passport / ID Type\*, Passport / ID Number\*, Student ID\*, University\*, Major\*, Year of Study\*, passport bio-page image\*, Proof of Enrolment (attachment)\*, Email\*, Phone / WhatsApp. The header at L10 reads "*Please provide an image of the passport bio‑page."

**Declaration:** information is true; entry is original; all members are 18 or older (minors attach parental or guardian consent); and, if awarded, the team will cooperate with identity and enrolment verification **before results are announced, failing which the award is forfeited.** Team leader's signature and date.

**How to send:** email the form to the competition mailbox with subject `International Track Registration – Team Name – Team Leader Name`. A confirmation reply is promised within 48 hours.

### Problems inside the form itself

- Instruction 3 says "no scans are required at registration", but instruction 4 asks for an enrolment scan by email, instruction 9 asks for a passport bio-page image, and the table marks both attachments required (\*). Read it as: **attach both scans**. Keep them out of the repo and send them only to the organiser mailbox.
- The form asks for IDs and passports, so it is personal data. Send it once, to the organiser address only, never in a public issue or a shared chat.
- Instruction 7 says the proposal summary is "for the preliminary screening": it is read by the screeners before anything else. Fill it in even though it is optional, using the one-line pitch from the pitch docs.
- The signature line means a signed or typed-name acknowledgment from the team leader. Ask the organisers whether a typed name is enough; sign a PDF if in doubt.
